The Walmart Site and App Experience


Order everything from groceries to gadgets using Walmart.com or the Walmart app. Take advantage of the new features that make shopping simpler while saving you time and money.

How to get your items
You have three ways to order your items:

Pickup: Pickup your order curbside at a nearby store.
Delivery: We'll bring your order to your home from a store. (Fees may apply)
Shipping: Your order will be shipped fast via UPS, FedEx, or another carrier.
Pickup and Delivery from store
Select a nearby store and timeslot to pick up your items, or we can deliver your order right to your home.

How it works:

Sign into your Walmart account.
On the homepage, select How do you want your items?
Under How do you want your items select Pickup or Delivery.
Select Delivery to check to see if your address can receive Delivery services.
If your address is within range of a store that offers Delivery services, the website or app will list those stores. You can choose a store from the list and then select Save As My Store.
If no stores are listed, the address is not within range of Delivery services from a store. You must choose Shipping or Pickup to place an order.
Reserve a Pickup/Delivery time.
Add items to your order.
Not all items are available for Pickup and Delivery.
Select Checkout under the Pickup and delivery options section of your cart.
Select Checkout all items to place an order for all items, both Pickup/Delivery and shipping.
Check out by the time displayed to keep your reservation.
If you order an item that isn’t available for pickup and delivery from your local store, we’ll ship it to you. We group shipped items separately in your cart and they ship separately from your reserved pickup or delivery order. Look for availability info on the item page and search results page.

Out of Stock Items


If an item isn’t available, it will show out-of-stock.

Unfortunately, sometimes an item may show available when you place an order, but by the time we process the order, the item is no longer in stock. If this happens, we'll email you to let you know that we canceled the item or the order. We restock items as quickly as we can.



Quantity Limits and Bulk Purchases



Some items have limits on the amount we can ship to you. If you can't add more of an item to your cart, it's either due to quantity limits or stock availability. Unfortunately, we can't send more than the quantity limit allows.

Walmart.com reserves the right to limit the quantity of items purchased per person, household, or order. These restrictions may apply to orders placed by the same Walmart account; using the same credit card, gift card, and/or checking account; and orders that use the same billing and/or shipping address.

Our website is meant for personal, non-commercial use. Since our items are already at everyday low prices, we generally don't offer a discount on bulk purchases. We reserve the right to prohibit dealers or resellers.



Price and Other Listing Errors



Listing errors are incorrect details about the product in item listings.

Some of the product details that may contain errors are:

The item’s price
The images or description (color, size, features, etc.)
Quantity or what’s included
If we catch a listing error while processing your order, we'll cancel your order.

Canceling your order is the last thing we want to do, but it may be necessary when we find listings with incorrect details.
If we need to cancel your order, we'll send you an email with the reason for cancellation.
We reserve the right to cancel item(s) and orders in accordance with our Terms of Use.

Food Safety


Walmart ensures the safety of our food through:

Maintaining clean facilities
We have routine sanitation schedules for all facilities and equipment.
We have our stores regularly inspected by local, state, and federal agencies and third party food safety auditors.
Associate training which includes:
Understanding food safety and quality.
Maintaining required standards for hygiene, cleanliness, cooking, proper temperature control, and proper bagging.
Bagging raw proteins in meat bags separately from other foods. This aims to prevent cross-contamination.
Bagging chemicals separately from all foods.
Time and temperature requirements for food safety.
Proper product management
For recalled products, we:
Work quickly to block the item from selling.
Remove it from our store’s shelves (including pickup & delivery).
Visit our corporate site for more information about how recalled products impact our facilities.

Food Safety Tips
To keep your food safe and fresh:

Place all chilled and/or frozen products in the refrigerator or freezer as soon as possible.
If using contactless delivery, retrieve and stow your order as soon as possible.
Place raw meat and seafood on the bottom shelf.
Avoid direct contact of raw meats/seafood with ready-to-eat foods.
Always wash whole produce thoroughly before you eat it.
Thoroughly cook foods to a recommended, safe internal temperature.
Place any leftovers in the refrigerator within two hours of cooking.
Date Labeling
Best If Used By: This date shows when the item is at peak quality (as suggested by the product supplier/manufacturer).
While it's not an expiration date, it’s a good indication of the quality of the product.
Use By or Freeze By: This date shows a product that is time or temperature-sensitive.
You should either use or place the product in your freezer by this date.
Fresh-sliced Deli Labels
You may see today’s date on a fresh-sliced deli meat or cheese item. This is the date the meat or cheese was sliced, not an expiration date.



Online Vision Center Orders


You can now order glasses and frames on Walmart.com and contacts on Walmartcontacts.com! We ship the items to your home, and they arrive within 7-14 business days.

Placing an Order
Login into your Walmart.com account.
Search for eyeglasses and add your frames to the cart.
Select Options to customize.
Selecting eligible frames will show the prompt Make these frames your own.
Select the prescription type, materials, and lens add-ons.
Review your selection and select Add prescription.
You'll see a prompt to sign in (if needed) or a prompt to create a new Vision profile (if the prescription is for someone else).
Enter your pupillary distance (PD).
This is the distance in millimeters between the centers of your pupils.
You can enter it manually or select Measure Now to have your camera measure it for you.
Select Add to cart.
You can continue adding items until you're ready to check out.
Check out.
You'll get a confirmation email when you place the order and another when it ships.
Returns and Refunds
You can return your glasses in the Walmart app. They're eligible for refund to your original payment method only within the 60-day return window. They're not eligible for replacement.

Go to the Order Details page.
Select Start a Return.
Select the item(s) to return.
Select the item issue and item condition.
Select the return method
Review the order and select Submit.
Common Questions
What information do I need to place a Vision order?

You need a valid, current prescription and a PD measurement. Prescription requirements vary by state. You can measure your PD on Walmart.com.

Can I use a prescription for contact lenses to order glasses?

Contact lenses and glasses lenses are different prescriptions. See Walmart Contacts for more information.

How will I be notified if there's a problem with my prescription or order?

You'll get an email or text within 48 hours letting you know that the order has canceled.

Can I order progressive or bifocal lenses?

No, only single-vision lenses are available at this time. Progressive and bifocal lenses will be available in the future.

What if I have questions about a frame fit or style?

Please contact your local Walmart Vision Center for help.

What if my frames need adjustment?

Please visit your local Walmart Vision Center for free adjustments.

Can I use insurance benefits or insurance reimbursement?

We don't accept insurance for online eyewear orders at this time. We do accept most major insurance in the Vision Centers. Please contact your local Walmart Vision Center or your insurance provider for more information.

How can I find my order confirmation or track my order?

View your order in your purchase history or use the Walmart app to track your package.

Is there a warranty on my frames?

There is a 60-day satisfaction guarantee with online Vision orders. This doesn't apply to eyewear from other departments, such as Sporting Goods or Apparel/Accessories. You can also buy a 12-month replacement guarantee at a Walmart Vision Center within 14 days of your purchase. The guarantee isn't available in:

Florida
Puerto Rico
