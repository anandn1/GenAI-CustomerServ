Substitutions for Store Pickup and Delivery Items
If an item you ordered is out of stock, we'll offer a similar item as a substitution. You have the choice to accept, decline, or return substituted items. We'll charge you the price of any substituted item(s). You’ll notice a temporary authorization hold. This is the amount we authorize to cover weighted items, replacements, and any bag fees in your state.

Choosing Substitutions
If your ordered item isn't available, we'll send an email and offer a similar item. There is a limited amount of time to respond. The email will give you time to accept or refuse the substitution(s).

If you don't accept or decline the item offered, we'll substitute it. If you're unhappy with the substitution, you can start a return from Purchase History on the Walmart app or Walmart.com.

Selecting Substitutions During Checkout 
Before placing your order, you can choose the items that you don’t want substituted. You can select the items you prefer as a substitution. If an item on your order is out of stock and you picked a substitution, we’ll replace it with the one you chose. If the item isn’t in stock and you let us know you don’t want us to substitute it, we'll remove it from your order.

Substitutions on SNAP/EBT orders
For orders placed with SNAP/EBT, you can choose substitutions and we’ll charge you the price of the item based on the price of the item and the payment method.
If you don’t select a backup method of payment for the order but allow for substitutions:

We'll substitute with an item that is the same or lower priced as the original item.
If an alternate item is not available, the original item will be canceled, and we’ll refund your SNAP/EBT card.
If you select a backup method of payment for the order and we must substitute with an item of higher price:

Any difference in cost for your substitution will be charged to the alternate payment method.
Amounts charged to non-SNAP/EBT payment methods are subject to sales tax.
Reject Substitutions During Pickup
If you don't want the substitution, simply let the associate at the store know, and they'll remove it from your order.

Reject Substitutions During Delivery
Delivery drivers can't remove the item from your order and return it to the store. You’ll need to start a return from your Purchase History on the Walmart app or Walmart.com.

Canceled Orders
Sometimes we cancel orders to save you time and money. If we cancel your order, we won’t charge you.

You may see a temporary hold on your credit or debit card account associated with a canceled order. Your bank may have placed a temporary hold on that amount when your order was placed and will remove the temporary hold based on their policies and procedures. We cannot remove the temporary hold. Please contact your bank with any questions regarding any temporary hold. It is sometimes called an authorization hold and shows as pending in your account. 

We may have canceled your order because:
it exceeded our rules for quantity limits and bulk purchases.
an item is out of stock.
our webpage had price and other listing errors.
our Credit and Fraud Avoidance department needs more information.
a carrier or shipping method was unavailable.
you were using an older browser or app version that‘s no longer supported by Walmart.
You’ll get an email if we canceled any part of your order, or if we need more information to process your order.

Alcohol Orders
You can include alcohol in your delivery and/or pickup order if state or local laws allow it.

You'll find alcohol items under the Grocery department in the Alcohol category.

Reasons You Won’t Get Your Alcohol Order 
You’re under 21 years of age.
You can't provide a valid government-issued photo identification.
For delivery orders, the entire order will be returned to the store if you can't show a valid identification.
For pickup orders, the alcohol will be removed from your order if you can’t show a valid identification.
You will not be charged for the removed alcohol.
You appear intoxicated.
You appear to be giving alcohol to someone under 21.
Alcohol Sales Hours Vary 

Some states and local laws may prevent the sale of alcohol during certain business hours.

Orders with alcohol will only be delivered during alcohol sales hours. 
If you try to pick up your alcohol order outside the legal hours to sell alcohol, you’ll have to wait until the legal hours or the alcohol will be removed from your order. 
ID Requirements
You'll be asked to show a valid government-issued photo identification for all orders with alcohol.

Pickup orders:
The curbside associate will ask for your photo identification. 
They’ll enter your birthday into their device to complete age verification.  
Delivery orders:
The delivery driver will ask for your photo identification. 
They’ll use their delivery driver app to scan your identification.
InHome orders:
The InHome associate will ask for your photo identification. 
They’ll use their device to scan your identification.

Pickup and Delivery Changes and Exceptions
How do I change my Pickup time?
You can change your store pick up time if the order is still in process, and it is before the cutoff time. The cutoff time will appear as a banner at the top of your purchase history. Before the cutoff time, you can:

Add or remove items.
Increase or decrease quantities.
Allow or disallow substitutions.
Reschedule the time slot (only once).
How do I change my order from Delivery to Pickup or, Pickup to Delivery?
You can’t change the fulfillment type after you place your order and checkout. The only exception is if you get a text message about a delayed scheduled delivery that gives this option.

If you have checked out and then want to change from Pickup to Delivery, or from Delivery to Pickup, you must request to cancel and then place a new order.

What if I am late to my scheduled Pickup?
If you are late to your scheduled Pickup, and the Pickup window is prior to 8 p.m., you can still check in by 8 p.m. local time on the Pickup date.
If you are late to check in for your Pickup order and it is after 8 p.m., you should reschedule or cancel and reorder.
Frozen and perishable items such as produce are stored at the correct temperature until you pick up your order.
If you don’t pick up the order by the end of the day, the store cancels your order the next day. This releases the authorization hold on your payment method.
You can reschedule your timeslot, if needed.
Select the reschedule button on the Order Details page.

Temporary Holds and Charges
What is a temporary hold?

When we authorize an estimated order amount from the credit or debit card, the bank places a hold on that amount to ensure there are enough funds to complete the purchase. It’s sometimes called an authorization hold and shows as pending in your account.

Why is my temporary hold different from my order total?

The hold may be more than the order’s final charge for many reasons:

Items priced by weight 
Substitutions 
Change in the number of items
Any order cancellations
Bag fees, depending on the state
When will I be charged my final order total?

We charge the final order total to your credit or debit card once your order is picked up, delivered, or shipped.

When will the temporary hold to be removed?

Once the order is complete, the bank will remove the hold based on their policies. This can take up to 10 days after the order is complete to remove the hold.

We can’t remove the hold, only the bank can. Please contact your bank with any other questions.

What is an order adjustment debit?

When your payment method includes a prepaid or EBT card, an order adjustment debit is charged to make sure there are enough funds to complete your purchase.

How long does it take to get a refund for an order adjustment debit?

This charge covers adjustments to your final order. Once your final total is confirmed, you'll be refunded any amount that is left over. Refunds to an EBT, gift card or refund credit should be refunded within three hours.

Why am I seeing two authorization charges on my account for an online order?

This can happen if there’s an issue with the pickup or delivery time slot of your order. If it took longer than 15 minutes to complete checkout after selecting your timeslot, and you had to pick a new timeslot, you may see two temporary authorization holds.

This can also happen if you add items to your order after it’s initially placed. You may see a new hold for the additional items. 

Please contact your bank if you need more help with authorization holds.

Why am I seeing a $.28 fee on my delivery?

The state of Colorado has a retail delivery fee on all deliveries made by a motor vehicle. Colorado requires us to charge this delivery fee within the state for any item(s) that are subject to sales tax.

We can’t refund this charge. We can only reverse it if the order is canceled, and the delivery doesn’t occur.

For more details, you can visit the Official Colorado State website, Retail Delivery Fee﻿.

Why am I seeing a $0.50 fee on my delivery? 

The state of Minnesota has a retail delivery fee of $0.50 on all retail deliveries where charges for applicable items equals or exceeds $100. 

We can't refund this charge. We can only reverse it if the order is cancelled, and/or the delivery doesn't occur. 

For more details, you can visit the Official Minnesota State website, Retail Delivery Fee.

Flexible Spending Accounts and Health Savings Accounts
You can use your Flexible Spending Accounts (FSH) and Health Savings accounts (HSA) in store or on Walmart.com.

What is a Flexible Spending Account (FSA)?
A Flexible Spending Account (FSA) can help you save money. It is a special account offered by some employers that lets you set aside money before taxes to pay for health-related and medical expenses, including selecting over the counter (OTC) products.

In general, you must use most of the money in your FSA by the end of the year, or you might lose it.

What is a Health Savings Account (HSA)?
Like an FSA, a Health Savings Account (HSA) is a type of savings account that allows you to set aside pre-tax dollars to pay for certain health-related and medical expenses, like over the counter (OTC) products. However, you need to have a special type of health insurance called a high-deductible health plan to use an HSA. Check with your health plan to see if your plan is eligible for an HSA. 

In general, you don’t have to spend all the money in your HSA by the end of the year. It rolls over and stays with you, even if you change jobs.
