Track Your Order
You can track your order from your shipping confirmation email or by visiting Walmart.com.

When your package ships, we'll send you an email with the tracking link. You can use the link at any time to check its progress.

Track your order on Walmart.com:
Log into your Walmart.com account. 
On Walmart.com, select Account. 
Select Purchase history.
Select Track Shipment.
Track your order on the Walmart app:
Open the Walmart app.
Select Account.
Select Purchase history.
Scroll to the order you want to track and select Track Shipment.

Edit or Cancel an Order
You can edit or request cancellation for most items and orders before shipment in Purchase history.

Edit Items in an Order 
You can edit your pickup and delivery orders up to 3 hours and 15 minutes before your scheduled timeslot begins. You can edit any other orders for items sold by Walmart while they’re being prepared.

During your edit window, you can:    

Add or remove items.
Increase or decrease quantities.
Allow or disallow substitutions.
To edit an item on your order for items sold by Walmart:

Log into your Walmart.com account.
Select Account.
Select Purchase history.
Select Edit items.
For items sold by Walmart, we may charge shipping, delivery, or pickup fees for orders under $35 and remove those fees if the total order value of the items sold by Walmart exceeds $35. Edits to your order may take the order total over or under the $35 threshold.

Make other changes to your order  
While your order for items sold by Walmart is being prepared, you can:  

Reschedule your timeslot
Choose a different store location
Edit your shipping address
Change your payment method
To reschedule an order: 

Log into your Walmart.com account.
Select Account.
Select Purchase history.
Find the order and select View details.
Select Reschedule.
You can only reschedule an order once.
If there's a delay in your scheduled delivery, you might get a text message or push notification giving you the option to change your delivery order to a store pickup. You can choose a time slot within the next 24 hours from when the notification was received. If you choose to pick up your order, we'll refund the delivery fee and tip amount that was on your order.

Cancel your order 
To request an order cancellation:

Log into your Walmart.com account.
Select Account.
Select Purchase history.
Find the order you want to cancel.
Scroll to the bottom and select Request Cancellation, if available.
Edit Options Unavailable
You might not have options to edit or cancel your order because:

Your order is on its way to you.
You ordered items from a Marketplace seller.
We are unable to cancel your Marketplace order.
If you can't edit or cancel your order, you can return most items. For more information, review the Walmart Return Policy﻿, the Marketplace return policy, or Start a Return Online once you’ve received the item.


Substitutions for Store Pickup and Delivery Items
If an item you ordered is out of stock, we'll offer a similar item as a substitution. You have the choice to accept, decline, or return substituted items. We'll charge you the price of any substituted item(s). You’ll notice a temporary authorization hold. This is the amount we authorize to cover weighted items, replacements, and any bag fees in your state.

Choosing Substitutions
If your ordered item isn't available, we'll send an email and offer a similar item. There is a limited amount of time to respond. The email will give you time to accept or refuse the substitution(s).

If you don't accept or decline the item offered, we'll substitute it. If you're unhappy with the substitution, you can start a return from Purchase History on the Walmart app or Walmart.com.

Selecting Substitutions During Checkout 
Before placing your order, you can choose the items that you don’t want substituted. You can select the items you prefer as a substitution. If an item on your order is out of stock and you picked a substitution, we’ll replace it with the one you chose. If the item isn’t in stock and you let us know you don’t want us to substitute it, we'll remove it from your order.

Substitutions on SNAP/EBT orders
For orders placed with SNAP/EBT, you can choose substitutions and we’ll charge you the price of the item based on the price of the item and the payment method.
If you don’t select a backup method of payment for the order but allow for substitutions:

We'll substitute with an item that is the same or lower priced as the original item.
If an alternate item is not available, the original item will be canceled, and we’ll refund your SNAP/EBT card.
If you select a backup method of payment for the order and we must substitute with an item of higher price:

Any difference in cost for your substitution will be charged to the alternate payment method.
Amounts charged to non-SNAP/EBT payment methods are subject to sales tax.
Reject Substitutions During Pickup
If you don't want the substitution, simply let the associate at the store know, and they'll remove it from your order.

Reject Substitutions During Delivery
Delivery drivers can't remove the item from your order and return it to the store. You’ll need to start a return from your Purchase History on the Walmart app or Walmart.com.

Canceled Orders
Sometimes we cancel orders to save you time and money. If we cancel your order, we won’t charge you.

You may see a temporary hold on your credit or debit card account associated with a canceled order. Your bank may have placed a temporary hold on that amount when your order was placed and will remove the temporary hold based on their policies and procedures. We cannot remove the temporary hold. Please contact your bank with any questions regarding any temporary hold. It is sometimes called an authorization hold and shows as pending in your account. 

We may have canceled your order because:
it exceeded our rules for quantity limits and bulk purchases.
an item is out of stock.
our webpage had price and other listing errors.
our Credit and Fraud Avoidance department needs more information.
a carrier or shipping method was unavailable.
you were using an older browser or app version that‘s no longer supported by Walmart.
You’ll get an email if we canceled any part of your order, or if we need more information to process your order.

Delayed Orders
We do our best to keep up with the growing demand for popular items. Sometimes, pre-transit order delays still happen. If an order is delayed, we'll send order update emails in real-time.

Pickup Delays
Our goal is to get your order to you as quickly as possible. Contact customer care after you’ve:

waited for the Ready for Pickup email.
called the phone number on the pickup sign to check in for your Walmart order.
If you’re experiencing an extended delay, contact customer care using the Chat with us button.

Delivery Delays
If there's a delay in your scheduled delivery, you may get a text message or push notification inviting you to change your delivery order to a pickup order. You can choose a pickup time slot within the next 24 hours. If you pick up your order, we'll refund your delivery fees and tip.

Select the Chat with us button to contact customer care if you haven’t received your order after the delivery window has passed.

Delivery Returned to Store
If your driver returns your delivery to store, an associate will contact you to reschedule your delivery. If we can’t reschedule it, we’ll cancel your delivery order and won’t charge you for it.

Drivers return orders when:

they can’t find your address.
no safe location is found.
you aren’t reachable.
you canceled your order.
to comply with food safety rules.

Follow the steps below if you didn't receive your items but got a delivery notification.

Look around the delivery location.
Check the delivery confirmation.

Review the address saved in your Walmart.com account. Make sure it doesn’t have any spelling errors or numerical mistakes.
Wait two business days to see if it arrives. It’s rare, but sometimes carriers may mark packages as delivered before they arrive.
If you still haven’t received it after two business days, select the Chat with us button.

If you have a delivery and your Purchase History shows your package was delivered, but you can't find it:

If it is the wrong house, check your address in Google Maps to see if the rooftop pin is correct.
Select the Chat with us button.

Alcohol Orders
You can include alcohol in your delivery and/or pickup order if state or local laws allow it.

You'll find alcohol items under the Grocery department in the Alcohol category.

Reasons You Won’t Get Your Alcohol Order 
You’re under 21 years of age.
You can't provide a valid government-issued photo identification.
For delivery orders, the entire order will be returned to the store if you can't show a valid identification.
For pickup orders, the alcohol will be removed from your order if you can’t show a valid identification.
You will not be charged for the removed alcohol.
You appear intoxicated.
You appear to be giving alcohol to someone under 21.
Alcohol Sales Hours Vary 

Some states and local laws may prevent the sale of alcohol during certain business hours.

Orders with alcohol will only be delivered during alcohol sales hours. 
If you try to pick up your alcohol order outside the legal hours to sell alcohol, you’ll have to wait until the legal hours or the alcohol will be removed from your order. 
ID Requirements
You'll be asked to show a valid government-issued photo identification for all orders with alcohol.

Pickup orders:
The curbside associate will ask for your photo identification. 
They’ll enter your birthday into their device to complete age verification.  
Delivery orders:
The delivery driver will ask for your photo identification. 
They’ll use their delivery driver app to scan your identification.
InHome orders:
The InHome associate will ask for your photo identification. 
They’ll use their device to scan your identification. 


When you get items delivered from the store to your home with Walmart delivery services, you can tip your driver and give feedback on your experience.

Keep in mind:
Tips are optional and 100% go to the driver.
You can choose to tip before or after placing your order or after delivery.
You’re free to edit your tip amount for up to three hours after delivery.
You can add an additional tip for your driver after three hours and up to 24 hours after your order is delivered.
You have up to 14 days to add a tip for your delivery driver if you didn’t tip when the order was placed.
To add a tip for items delivered from your local store:
Log into your Walmart.com account.  
Select Account.
Select Purchase History.
Select the appropriate order.
Select Add tip or Add extra tip.
If you don't see the option to add a tip, your order is not eligible.

We'll also send you a push notification from the app for you to give feedback and/or a tip after your delivery.

To leave feedback, select Give feedback.


Pickup and Delivery
You can take advantage of Walmart’s Everyday Low Prices and save time on your shopping with our pickup and delivery service.

Simply place an order online, choose a timeslot, and an associate will gather your items and place them in bags. When your order is ready, we'll send a Ready for Pickup email if you’ve chosen to pick up from your local store or your order can be delivered right to your door.

Pickup Orders
To check-in online or the app:

Open the app or email.
You can also use the link in your text message.
Select I’m on my way.
Once you’ve checked in, head to the store and park in one of the Curbside Pickup parking spots.
When you arrive, select I’ve parked.
Enter the spot number and color of the vehicle.
A Walmart associate will bring your order to you.
If you’re having issues checking in from the Walmart app, you can use the store check-in steps.

To check-in at the store:

Use one of the Curbside Pickup parking spots.
Call the phone number on the parking sign or in your confirmation email.
A Walmart associate will bring your order to you.
Delayed or Canceled Pickup Orders
Our goal is to get your order to you as quickly as possible.

Before you contact customer care for a delayed or cancel order, be sure to:

Wait for the Ready for Pickup notification.
Use our check-in option before you leave for the store and when you arrive.
Call the store if you've received the notification and checked in before you arrived and are still experiencing a delay. You'll find the phone number in our app or when you arrive at the pickup location.

Select the Chat with us button for any other concerns about wait times.

Delivery of Your Order
A local, third-party delivery driver will bring your order right to your door. After the driver picks up your order, you'll get a notification letting you know the driver is on their way. In some cases, the delivery can take up to two hours to get to you.

If you select, I’ll sign for my order, this will require you to provide a pin or signature when the driver delivers your order.
A pin may also be required for certain orders or high value items. You’ll be notified at checkout if this is required.

Once the order is out for delivery, the four-digit passcode will show under the tracking status bar under Order Details. You can also view the pin when selecting Track Order.
If your order is being delivered to someone else, you’ll need to provide the recipient with the pin for a successful delivery.
If you're satisfied with your delivery experience, you can leave feedback and add a tip for your driver.
