Tips for Buying a Major Appliance
To help avoid returns and/or restocking fees, we recommend that you use these tips when buying a major appliance:

Research your options.
Measure the space at least twice and compare to dimensions included on the item page to ensure fit.
Make sure the space has the right utilities, voltage, and plug type for your appliance.
Buy any necessary accessories for installation/use (E.g., hoses, plugs, vents, etc.).
Develop a plan to assemble and install the item.
Share any special delivery instructions with the delivery carrier (where applicable).
Accepting a Major Appliance Delivery
Before accepting a major appliance delivery, you should inspect your appliance for defects/damage when it arrives.

If any damage exists, please notify the driver, ask for options, and/or refuse the delivery.
Discounts for defects/damage are at the MP seller’s discretion, we recommend refusing delivery to avoid further complications.
Returns and Refunds
You can get a full refund for an unopened/uninstalled major appliance within two days of delivery.

Start the return in the Walmart app or on our returns page.
Major appliances aren’t returnable to Walmart stores.
Unopened Appliances
Unopened appliances are unopened, undamaged, and uninstalled appliances in original factory sealed packaging.

You can return these items to the seller within 30 days of accepting delivery in the original manufacturer packaging.
Unopened/uninstalled major appliances are subject to a restocking fee of up to 20% after the two-day time frame.
Opened/Installed Appliances
An open/installed product refers to an item that has been received, inspected, and signed for by you, and for which the packaging/seal has been broken. You should inspect your items before accepting delivery, signing for it, and before opening the item. If you notice any damage before accepting and signing, the delivery can be refused at no charge.

Exchanges and refunds can occur within the 2-day timeframe.
For the remaining 28 days returns/refunds for opened items are at the seller’s discretion and may be subject to additional fees.
Installed items cannot be returned and customers should defer to the manufacturer warranty.
Exceptions
You can return small or dorm size appliances and wine refrigerators within 90 days.
You can return them within 30 days of delivery if sold on Walmart MP.
Any custom or made-to-order items cannot be returned for refund or exchange.

When you follow the steps to add Pharmacy Access online, the personal details entered must match what your local pharmacy store has saved in their system. If any data is incorrect or doesn’t match, you’ll see one of these errors:

Sorry, we couldn't verify your identity. You can try again in 24 hours.
We couldn't verify your identity.
To troubleshoot pharmacy access errors, follow these steps:

Part 1: Refresh your device/account settings.

Sign out of the Walmart.com website and Walmart app.
Open a new private or incognito window in your web browser or use a new browser that hasn’t accessed Walmart.com before.
Go to your web browser's Settings/Preferences.
Clear the browser cache and history.
Add Pharmacy Access:

Sign in to the Walmart Pharmacy website with your Walmart.com registered email.
Select Add Pharmacy Access.
Do not select Create a new account.
Enter your personal information exactly as it’s saved at your local pharmacy.
To confirm your personal details on file, call your local pharmacy.
Select Continue and complete the verification process.
Don’t exceed this step more than twice in 24 hours.
If you still see an error, continue to Part 2.
Part 2: Contact your local pharmacy store.

Call your local Walmart Pharmacy store where you fill your medications.
Confirm all information below is saved in the store's system to match what you enter online:
First and last name
Date of birth
Phone number
Current address
Walmart.com sign-in email
Confirm that the pharmacy has only one pharmacy profile created for you.
If you have more than one profile, the pharmacy must merge all profiles into one before you can move forward.
Once you update your details, repeat the steps in Part 1.
If you still see an error, continue to Part 3.
Part 3: Verify in person at your local store.

Bring your photo identification to your local Walmart Pharmacy store.
Inform the pharmacy that you can’t add pharmacy access online due to an error.
Ask the pharmacy to verify your identification in person using their store system.
If the pharmacy isn’t familiar with this request, ask for help from the pharmacy manager.
Show your photo identification so the pharmacy can find your pharmacy profile in the store system.
Confirm that the pharmacy has only one pharmacy profile created for you.
If the pharmacy finds more than one profile, they must merge all profiles into one before you can move forward.
Confirm all info below is saved in the store's system to match what you enter online:
First and last name
Date of birth
Phone number
Address
Sign-in email
Once your identification is verified in the system, you’ll receive an email with instructions.
Open the email from Walmart Pharmacy when you receive it.
Select the Get started button in the email.
Verify your personal details to complete verification.
Once verification is complete, your pharmacy profile will be connected to your Walmart.com account
If you can’t verify your identification at your pharmacy store, continue to Part 4.
Part 4: Contact Customer Care.

Call 1-800-WALMART

Find the Right Glasses for You

To find the right glasses for your face shape, use the descriptions below.

Oval face: Balanced features and gentle curves.

Oval faces can wear nearly any frame style.
To add a little pizzazz, try square and rectangular frames, which contrast with your face shape.
Consider frames that are not too large or too small or a natural balance of your features.
Oblong face: Elongated, tapers toward the chin.

Look for deep frame styles with low temple attachments.
Consider frames that are not too narrow to balance your features.
Round face: Width and length are about the same, with the widest point at the cheeks.

Look for square, rectangular, and angular frames.
Angular frame shapes play off your fuller cheeks and rounded chin.
Also try shapes that upsweep at the corners to emphasize your eyes.
Square face: Straight lines, square chin, and broad forehead.

Look for oval and round frames.
Choose frames with soft, rounded edges to balance the lines of your face, while allowing your features to shine.
Triangular face: Narrower forehead with wider cheeks and jawline.

Try aviator style frames that are wider at the temples and narrow toward the chin.
Try frames that add width to the forehead and soften the jawline.
Diamond face: Very angular and wider at the temples.

Look for softly curved frames that are no wider than the cheekbones.
Try frames that are not too thick or decorative at the temples.
Heart face: Strongly tapers toward the chin with a wider forehead.

Look for frames that balance the different widths of your facial features.
For example, bottom-heavy frames balance the narrowness of the lower half of your face, while oval shapes can soften a more pointed chin.
Choose eyeglass colors that complement your skin undertone, hair color, and eye color

Knowing what colors suit your skin tone will also help you find the perfect frames.

Walmart Pet Prescription Process

Walmart Pet Prescriptions is an online pharmacy that allows you to order pet medications and vet diet food on Walmart.com with your vet's approval.

How To Shop Pet Prescriptions
Begin shopping for pet prescriptions by searching for items individually or by going to Services > Pharmacy > Pet Pharmacy to browse. 
Items can also be found under Account > Pets > Pet Pharmacy.
Add the item(s) to your cart.
Enter your pet and vet information.
We'll contact the vet to verify the prescription. 
With approval, we'll fill the prescription. RX orders will ship to your home after authorization from the vet.
If the vet doesn't respond within two business days, we'll contact them again. It can take up to five business days for the vet to respond. If we can't verify the prescription with the vet, we'll contact you. If you no longer wish to wait, you can cancel the order if it's still in Placed status or request a cancelation if it's in Preparing status. 

Pet Prescription Substitutions
Pet Prescription orders may have a substitution if the vet makes a change to the prescription. These changes can be:

Item (E.g., Simparica Trio instead of Simparica)
Strength (5 mg instead of 3 mg)
Dosage (E.g., Instead of one pill ordered, vet approved 15 pills)
Weight (E.g., Simpartica for 11 lbs dog instead of five lbs.)
If the change results in a price increase:

10% or less price increase (and the item has not been changed by vet): We automatically approve the change.
Over 10% price increase or item changed by the vet: You must approve the change.
We’ll notify you via SMS, app notification, and/or email.
If you decline the change or do not respond by the deadline, the order will be canceled.
Refills
After placing a Walmart pet prescription order, whether a one-time purchase or a subscription, you’ll be notified of how many refills you have remaining via email. You will also be able to find this information in your pet prescription management page. If you do not have any refills, you won’t be able to set up a subscription for the prescription.

Reminder Notifications
We’ll remind you when your pet prescription is ready to refill so you won’t run out of medication. You'll get refill reminders through:

Email
Subject: It’s time to refill prescriptions
Walmart app push notifications
Subject: Refill your pet prescription
We only send reminders when there are available refills. Reminders are sent 7 to 10 days before the refill is due. If there is pending refill we won’t send the reminder.

To place a refill order:

Email: Select Refill now.
App notification: Select the notification to view details.

Expired or Out of Refills
If you would like to reorder an expired prescriptions or renew a prescription, you can add the item to your cart and select Reorder now on your prescription management page. We’ll contact your vet to approve it.

Vet Initiated Orders
If you have an existing PetRX order, your vet can add recommend prescriptions to your account. If you agree with the recommendations, you can add these items to our cart and check out.
